Recorded volume

Volume variation is the gap between louder and quieter recording frames within recognized words (90th–10th percentile), excluding very quiet frames. This updated measurement is not directly comparable to older saved volume ranges. Microphone distance, automatic gain and background noise affect it. It does not measure confidence or vocal expression.

Detected filler sounds

Counts spellings such as “um”, “uh”, “erm” and “hmm” in the original transcript. Whisper may omit these sounds; other languages and filler phrases are not counted. Editing the transcript does not change these counts.
